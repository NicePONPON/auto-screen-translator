## Auto Screen Translator for Windows

Auto-built from the latest source on main.

### How to install
1. Download AutoScreenTranslator-Windows.zip below
2. Extract the zip to any folder, e.g. C:\AutoScreenTranslator\
3. Double-click AutoScreenTranslator.exe - that is all

### First launch
On your first Capture, EasyOCR downloads ~180 MB of model files in the background.
Models are cached at %APPDATA%\ocr_translator\models after that - every launch is instant.
